# Jingling Journeys

Give your world a little more Holiday Cheer, by spreading it yourself with your own sleigh and team!

Build your sleighs, craft your toys, and even make some sugary treats to get you through the long night, and, with the magic you craft, take to the skies and deliver joy and whimsy to ALL in the minecraft world!

Or, if you prefer a more laid back approach, take a ride on the chill side in the mod's selection of sleighs, with any creature you desire to be hitched up { disclosure; not all creatures can be hitched to all kinds of sleighs }.

This mod is a labor of love to the season of Christmas, and hopes to give all who use it a jolly good time.
